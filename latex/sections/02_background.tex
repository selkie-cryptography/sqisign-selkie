\section{Background}
\label{sec:background}

We assume familiarity with elliptic curves and isogenies at the level
of the SQIsign specification~\cite{sqisign-spec-v2.0.1}, but not with the
internals of theta-coordinate arithmetic or quaternion ideal algorithms.
Where the spec is unclear, we say so; where the C reference makes an
undocumented choice, we document it here.
