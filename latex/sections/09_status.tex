\section{Current Status}
\label{sec:status}

\paragraph{v3.0 migration.}
The main branch is moving to the round-3
specification~\cite{sqisign-spec-v3.0}: NIST-I with
$p = 3\cdot 2^{324}-1$, $f = 324$, $\lambda = 128$.  The C reference is
pinned at its \texttt{nist-v3} tag; where the specification text and
the reference disagree on bytes, the reference wins and the divergence
is documented at the call site.  The port proceeds by layer, bottom
up.  The v2 protocol layer was removed first so that the test suite
stays green at every commit; it returns, rewritten, in the final
phase.  Table~\ref{tab:v3-phases} tracks the phases.

\begin{table}[h]
  \centering
  \begin{tabular}{lll}
    \toprule
    Phase & Scope & State \\
    \midrule
    0 & C reference pin, protocol layer removed & done \\
    1 & Fields: \texttt{Fp55}, \texttt{Fp64}, \texttt{Fp29x4}, \texttt{Fp26x4}, $\mathbb{F}_{p^2}$ & done \\
    2 & Fixed-precision integers ($30 \times 64$-bit limbs) & next \\
    3 & SHAKE256 PRNG and challenge hash, v3 encodings & pending \\
    4 & Curves: six-limb scalars, cofactors, hints, normalization & pending \\
    5 & Quaternions: inert ideals, integer-only lattice reduction & pending \\
    6 & Surfaces: Dartois--Duparc theta formulas, chain length $f-2$ & pending \\
    7 & Qlapoti ideal-to-isogeny & pending \\
    8 & Protocol: key generation, signing, verification, KATs & pending \\
    9 & Constant time, performance, documentation & pending \\
    \bottomrule
  \end{tabular}
  \caption{v3.0 migration phases.}
  \label{tab:v3-phases}
\end{table}

\paragraph{v2.0.1 baseline (tag \texttt{v2.0.1-final}).}
Baseline interoperability with the SQIsign v2.0.1 specification and
the reference C implementation was achieved and is preserved at the
tag.  That implementation produces byte-identical key pairs on every
\emph{known-answer test} (KAT) vector at the NIST-I parameter set
($103$ key-generation vectors), and signatures that verify on every
signing KAT ($100$ vectors).  Signature \emph{byte}-equality with the
reference holds on all $100$ signing KATs, verified by a byte-interop
cross-test harness that drives a live C reference over both the baked
vectors and fresh seeds (\S\ref{sec:testing}).  It exercises the full
pipeline end-to-end: quaternion ideal arithmetic, the Deuring
correspondence, the $(2,2)$-isogeny chain on theta surfaces, and
Montgomery-curve verification.  That baseline is what the rest of this
paper documents reaching: the bugs surfaced from specification
ambiguity (\S\ref{sec:bugs}), the type-level invariants that prevented
entire bug classes (\S\ref{sec:types}), and the test infrastructure
that gates byte-equality with the C reference (\S\ref{sec:testing}).
Section~\ref{sec:post-baseline} documents the performance and
constant-time work done on top of it; the v3 port carries that work
forward layer by layer.
